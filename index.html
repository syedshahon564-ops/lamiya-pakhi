DANGER SHAWON

GRAPHIC DESIGNER • DINAJPUR, BANGLADESH

I didn't start as a designer.

I studied Marketing at one of the top universities in Bangladesh.
I was taught how brands think, how markets work, and how business speaks.

But something was missing. Understanding a brand wasn't enough for me. I wanted to build its visual soul.

So I stepped into Design.
I took my business foundation, merged it with raw creativity, and taught myself how to turn complex strategies into clean visual identities.

I learned the fundamentals,
challenged myself, practiced relentlessly,
and slowly built my own visual language.

Every project you see here represents
my commitment to becoming
a better designer—
one step at a time.

Built from curiosity, discipline, and the courage to start.

Dinajpur, BD • Open For Commissions
